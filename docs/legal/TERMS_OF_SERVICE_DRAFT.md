## 1. Acceptance

By creating an account or using Cyrlo Ride you agree to these Terms and the Privacy Policy.

## 2. What Cyrlo Ride is

Cyrlo Ride connects passengers with independent riders (tricycle or car) inside **active service zones**. We are a technology platform. Riders are not employees unless we say otherwise in writing.

## 3. Eligibility

- You must be able to enter a binding agreement under Ghana law.  
- You must use a phone number you control. OTP verifies access to that number.  
- Riders must complete identity / document checks we require (including Ghana Card where applicable) and be approved for the zone they serve.

## 4. Service area

Trips can only start from pickups inside an **active** zone polygon. The first pilot zone is **Kokobin**. Zones can change; we may activate or deactivate areas.

## 5. Accounts and roles

You choose passenger or rider flows as offered in the app. You are responsible for activity on your account. Do not share OTP codes.

## 6. Fees, tokens, and payments

- Passengers pay a **booking fee** (and any other fees shown in-app) via our payment provider (Paystack).  
- Riders use **tokens** purchased in-app; one token is used when a ride is assigned.  
- Prices and packs may change; the server catalog is authoritative.  
- Payments are processed by Paystack; card/mobile-money rules of your bank/wallet also apply.

## 7. Booking, matching, and cancellation

- After the fee succeeds, we search for nearby eligible riders for a limited time.  
- If no rider accepts in time, or you cancel during search, the booking fee is refunded per our refund policy.  
- After a rider accepts, cancellations follow the refund matrix (see §8).  
- Only the assigned rider starts and completes the trip.

## 8. Refunds (summary)

| Situation | Typical outcome |
| --- | --- |
| No rider found / you cancel during search | Booking fee refunded |
| Rider cancels after accept / cannot complete | Fee and token handled per matrix (refunds where specified) |
| You cancel after a rider accepted | **No automatic refund**; Support may review; Finance decides fee refunds using published criteria |
| Trip completed | **No refund** for normal trip quality complaints |

Full operational detail: `docs/legal/PILOT_REFUND_AND_DISPUTE_POLICY.md`.

## 9. Disputes

You may open a dispute in-app within **24 hours** after completion or cancellation. We may review GPS and status history. Completing a trip does not create a right to a refund except narrow payment-error cases described in the refund policy.

## 10. Acceptable use

No fraud, harassment, illegal goods/transport, fake GPS, multi-accounting abuse, or interfering with riders/passengers/ops. We may suspend accounts.

## 11. Liability (pilot)

Service is provided “as available.” To the extent allowed by Ghana law, we are not liable for rider/passenger conduct, traffic delays, or indirect losses. Nothing here limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

## 12. Changes

We may update these Terms. Material changes will be notified in-app or by notice. Continued use after the effective date means acceptance.
