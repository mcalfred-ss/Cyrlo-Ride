Cyrlo Ride Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026 · Operated by Eqostack

Related: Terms of Service

This Privacy Policy explains what Cyrlo Ride collects, why we collect it, how we use it, and your choices.

Who we are

Cyrlo Ride is built and operated by Eqostack (Ghana). Contact: eqostack@gmail.com · WhatsApp 0246304492.

What we collect

  • Account — phone number, role (passenger/rider), and session tokens for login.
  • Rider onboarding — details you submit, Ghana Card information (encrypted at rest), and vehicle photos/documents in private storage.
  • Location — GPS while using rider availability / trip features, and trip location logs for assigned trips (matching, safety, disputes).
  • Bookings and payments — pickup/destination, status history, Paystack references, and token ledger entries.
  • Device / notifications — push device tokens if you enable notifications.
  • Support — messages and dispute content you send us.

What we do not sell

We do not sell your personal information.

How we use your information

  • Provide OTP login and secure sessions
  • Match passengers and riders inside active zones
  • Process payments and refunds via Paystack
  • Verify riders and store documents privately
  • Prevent fraud and abuse; secure the service
  • Meet legal and accounting obligations

Sharing with service providers

We share data only as needed to run Cyrlo Ride, for example:

  • Paystack — payments
  • Vynfy — OTP / critical SMS
  • Bunny.net — private document storage and CDN delivery of authorized files
  • Hosting / maps providers — servers and map tiles as configured

These providers act on our instructions and should not use your data for their own marketing. Ghana Card images are not published on public open URLs.

Retention

  • Account data is kept while your account is active.
  • Booking and payment records are kept for financial, dispute, and accounting needs.
  • Trip GPS points are kept at least through the 24-hour dispute window.
  • OTP codes are short-lived and stored hashed, not as plaintext.
  • If you request account closure, we remove or anonymise personal data where practical, except records we must keep for legal, security, or accounting reasons.

Security

We use HTTPS, hashed OTPs, encrypted identity fields, role-based staff access, audit logs, and private object storage with short-lived signed download links for authorized staff. No method is 100% secure.

Your choices

  • Ask us to access or correct profile data
  • Request account closure
  • Disable location or notifications in your phone settings (core ride features may stop working)
  • Contact us with privacy questions

Children

Cyrlo Ride is not directed at children under 18. Do not use the service if you are under 18.

International processing

Infrastructure may run on servers or CDNs outside Ghana. We still apply this policy and use processors under contract.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version with an updated date.
