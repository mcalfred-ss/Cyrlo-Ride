2. What Cyrlo Ride is

Cyrlo Ride is a technology platform that connects passengers with independent riders (tricycle or car) inside active service zones. Riders are not Eqostack employees unless we say otherwise in writing.

3. Eligibility

  • You must be able to enter a binding agreement under Ghana law (18+).
  • You must use a phone number you control. We verify access with a one-time SMS code (OTP).
  • Riders must complete identity and document checks we require (including Ghana Card where applicable) and be approved for the zone they serve.

4. Service area

Trips can only start from pickups inside an active zone polygon. The first pilot zone is Kokobin. We may activate or deactivate zones as operations change.

You are responsible for activity on your account. Do not share OTP codes. We may suspend accounts for fraud, abuse, or safety risks.

6. Fees, tokens, and payments

  • Passengers pay a booking fee (and any other fees shown in-app) through Paystack.
  • Riders use tokens purchased in-app. One token is used when a ride is assigned.
  • Prices and packs may change; amounts shown in the App at checkout are authoritative.
  • We do not store your full card details; Paystack processes payments.

7. Booking and cancellation

  • After the booking fee succeeds, we search for nearby eligible riders for a limited time.
  • If no rider accepts in time, or you cancel during search, the booking fee is refunded per our refund rules.
  • After a rider accepts, refunds follow the matrix below — there is no automatic refund if you cancel after acceptance.
  • Only the assigned rider starts and completes the trip. Passenger “done” acknowledgement does not change trip status by itself.
